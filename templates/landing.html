What's inside

Six modules, working together like one brick in the wall.

Every part of a brick kiln operation touches every other part — who's on the floor today decides what gets produced, what gets produced decides what's paid, and what's paid depends on what's in stock. RM connects all of it, so nothing has to be re-typed, re-checked or reconciled by hand.

Employee Management

A single, verified record for every worker on the floor — from onboarding to daily status.

  • Aadhaar number + card photo, and notarized document upload per employee
  • Department assignment ties each worker to a piece-rate automatically
  • Active/Inactive status, searchable and exportable at any time

Department Management

The four roles of a brick line — Maker, Carrier, Converter, Transporter — each configured once.

  • Brick size (4"/6"/8"/9") set per department
  • Rate per 100 bricks drives payroll automatically, no manual entry
  • Live headcount per department at a glance

Storage & Production

What's made and what's stored, tracked together instead of in two separate systems.

  • Daily brick production logged per employee, per set, per brick size
  • Stock levels and locations update as production happens
  • Deductions & payments to suppliers tracked with reminder alerts

Payroll System

Salaries calculated the way brick kilns actually pay — by output, not by the clock.

  • Generate payroll directly from a month's production records
  • Advances tracked and auto-deducted from the next payout
  • Printable salary slips with full production & payment breakdown

Vehicle Management

Every truck, driver and trip — plus the paperwork that keeps them road-legal.

  • Insurance, road tax and PUC status per vehicle, in one view
  • Driver licensing and Aadhaar verification built in
  • Trip logs and maintenance history, fully searchable

Raw Materials

The supply side of the kiln — who you buy from, what you bought, and what's left.

  • Supplier directory with a searchable, always-current picker
  • Purchases automatically update material stock on save
  • Payment transactions by method — Online, Cash, RTGS, Cheque
